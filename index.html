Design rationale

Calm, credible, personal.

Today-first architecture

One primary action each day reduces decision fatigue and supports a natural habit.

Astrology as a core pillar

Cosmic Brief is the hero; Astrology and Tarot are equal hubs in the navigation.

Expert matching

Seven experts each have a distinct scope; every profile explains capabilities before chat begins.

Responsible AI

The AI badge is always visible. Copy avoids absolute predictions and emotional dependency.

Editorial mysticism

Expressive serif meets readable sans; a restrained mystical palette leaves generous breathing room.

Revenue with trust

No ads appear before a reading or inside chat; Premium appears only after a moment of value.
